Admissions

Begin your journey at Brightfield University. Learn about our admission requirements, application process, fees, and important dates.

Admission Requirements

Applicants seeking admission into Brightfield University must meet the following requirements:

  • Five credits in relevant subjects in WAEC, NECO, or equivalent.
  • A valid JAMB result for undergraduate applicants.
  • Applicants must meet the required score for their chosen program.
  • Applicants must provide all required documents during the application process.

How to Apply

  1. Choose your preferred program.
  2. Complete the online application form.
  3. Upload the required documents.
  4. Pay the application fee.
  5. Submit your application.
  6. Wait for the admission decision.

Tuition and Other Fees

Students are required to pay tuition and other applicable fees before the beginning of each academic session.

  • Tuition Fee
  • Registration Fee
  • Technology Fee
  • Library Fee
  • Medical Fee
  • Student Activities Fee
  • Accommodation Fee

Scholarships and Financial Aid

Brightfield University offers scholarships and financial assistance opportunities to eligible students based on academic performance, leadership, and other criteria.
