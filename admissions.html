Admissions

Begin your journey at Brightfield University. Learn about our admission requirements, application process, fees, and important dates.

Admission Requirements Details
O'Level Results (WAEC/NECO/GCE) Applicants must have a minimum of five (5) credits in relevant subjects, including English Language and Mathematics, obtained in not more than two sittings.
JAMB UTME Results Applicants must have a minimum score of 180 in the Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME) conducted by the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB).
JAMB Admission Letter Applicants must provide a copy of their JAMB admission letter indicating that they have been offered admission into Brightfield University with Brightfield University being your first choice.
Application Form Applicants must complete the online application form available on the Brightfield University website and submit it along with the required documents and application fee.
Passport Photograph Applicants must provide a recent passport-sized photograph (passport photograph) to be uploaded during the application process.
Birth Certificate or Declaration of Age Applicants must provide a copy of their birth certificate or a declaration of age from a recognized authority to verify their age.
Medical Certificate Applicants must provide a medical certificate from a recognized medical practitioner indicating that they are medically fit to pursue their studies at Brightfield University.
Letter of Recommendation Applicants may be required to provide one or more letters of recommendation from teachers, mentors, or other individuals who can attest to their academic abilities, character, and potential for success in higher education.
Transfer Students Transfer students must provide official transcripts from their previous institution and meet the academic requirements for transfer admission.

How to Apply

  1. Choose your preferred program.
  2. Complete the online application form.
  3. Upload the required documents.
  4. Pay the application fee.
  5. Submit your application.
  6. Wait for the admission decision.

Tuition and Other Fees

Students are required to pay tuition and other applicable fees before the beginning of each academic session.
New students are required to pay a non-refundable acceptance fee of ₦5,000 upon receiving their admission offer. They are to also pay a non-refundable registration fee of ₦10,000 upon registration. Tuition fees vary by program and are to be paid in full or in installments as per the university's payment schedule.

Other Fees

  1. Matriculation Fee: All new students to BrightField University are required to pay a sum of Thirty Thousand Naira only(₦30,000) into the University account.
  2. Medical Fee: All new students to BrightField University are required to pay medical fees. This allows 24 hours access to the healthcare centre, when needed.
  3. Student Representative Council(SRC) Dues: All students are members of the SRC and are expected to pay the sum of ₦5,000.00 as SRC dues
