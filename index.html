Brightfield University is a private university located in the heart of the city. It was founded by Nwankwo Chidinma Miracle in the year 2026.

Why choose Brightfield University?

Brightfield University is dedicated to providing a supportive and inclusive learning environment. Our experienced faculty, state-of-the-art facilities,
and diverse student body create a dynamic educational experience that prepares students for success in their chosen fields.

Our Programs

We offer a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various fields including Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Business. Our curriculum is designed to equip students with the skills and knowledge needed to succeed in their chosen careers.
